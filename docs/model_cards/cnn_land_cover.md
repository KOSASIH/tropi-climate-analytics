## Model Overview
ResNet-50-based convolutional neural network for multi-class land cover classification and deforestation change detection from Landsat-8/9 and Sentinel-2 multispectral imagery. Produces 10 m resolution land cover maps for 38 Indonesian provinces with automated deforestation alerts when annual forest loss exceeds 500 ha per district.

**Intended users:** KLHK (Ministry of Environment & Forestry), GEOSPATIAL agent, LAPAN, academic researchers.

## Training Data
| Source | Variable(s) | Resolution | Period |
|---|---|---|---|
| Landsat-8/9 OLI/TIRS | B1–B7 + TIRS | 30 m / 16-day | 2013–2025 |
| Sentinel-2 MSI | B2–B8A, B11, B12 | 10–20 m / 5-day | 2017–2025 |
| MODIS MCD12Q1 | Land cover type 1 (IGBP) | 500 m / annual | 2001–2025 |
| KLHK Ground Truth | Field-verified polygons (n=85,000) | — | 2020–2024 |
| OSM Indonesia | Urban footprint, road buffer | Vector | 2024 |

**Land Cover Classes (10):** Dense forest, secondary forest, shrubland, grassland/savanna, cropland, plantation (oil palm/rubber), wetland/mangrove, built-up area, bare land/mining, water body.
**Indonesian provinces covered:** All 38 provinces. Training patches: 2.4M image chips (256×256 px).
**Train/val/test split:** 70% / 15% / 15% stratified by province and class.

## Limitations & Risks
- **Cloud contamination:** Despite cloud masking, persistent cloud cover (Kalimantan wet season) reduces recall for forest classes by ~6%.
- **Small clearings:** Deforestation patches < 2 ha may fall below detection threshold at 30 m resolution.
- **Phenological variation:** Deciduous plantation mis-classification spikes ±5% in dry-season leaf-off periods.
- **Haze/smoke:** 2015/2019-scale peatfire smoke degrades classification accuracy by 8–12%.
- **Class imbalance:** Bare land/mining (< 2% of area) has higher false-negative rate.
- **Not intended for:** Legal enforcement decisions without independent field verification.
