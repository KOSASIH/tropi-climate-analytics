Following the Letter of Intent (LOI) previously submitted between **Tropi Climate Analytics** and the **Kementerian Lingkungan Hidup dan Kehutanan (KLHK)**, we write to formally advance the partnership toward a full data-sharing and co-monitoring agreement, with priority focus on deforestation change detection and national air quality surveillance.

### 1. Platform Overview

Tropi Climate Analytics delivers cloud-native climate intelligence powered by NASA satellite constellation data (Landsat-8/9, MODIS, VIIRS) fused with Indonesian ground network observations. Our deforestation and air quality modules directly complement KLHK's mandate for forest governance and environmental regulation:

- **Automated Deforestation Alerts**: Sub-weekly change detection at 30m resolution across all 34 Indonesian provinces using Landsat and MODIS time-series analysis
- **Air Quality Index (AQI) Monitoring**: Dual-source AQI (satellite MODIS aerosol + KLHK ground station fusion) per Indonesian ISPU standards (PERMENLHK P.14/2020)
- **Forest Fire Hotspot Tracking**: VIIRS-derived active fire detection with 375m resolution, updated every 6 hours
- **Land Cover Change Analytics**: Annual land cover transition matrices for KLHK forest category reporting (HP, HPT, HPK, HL, HK)

### 2. Proposed KLHK Contribution

Under the partnership framework (`docs/partnerships.md`), KLHK's proposed contributions include:

| Data Asset | Format | Delivery Mechanism | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Annual national land cover map (Peta Tutupan Lahan) | GeoTIFF/Shapefile | KLHK Open Data API | Annual |
| Air Quality Index station readings (AQMS) | JSON | KLHK Open Data API | Hourly |
| Deforestation ground truth verification reports | PDF/CSV | SFTP | Quarterly |
| Forest permit boundary data (PPKH) | Shapefile | KLHK Open Data API | As updated |

### 3. Platform Benefits to KLHK

Tropi Climate Analytics commits to:

- **KLHK Attribution** on all deforestation alerts and AQI products used in public reporting
- **SIPONGI+ Integration**: Providing VIIRS hotspot feeds directly to support KLHK's SIPONGI+ platform for fire control coordination
- **Annual Peta Deforestasi Nasional**: Co-producing the annual national deforestation map for KLHK's FREL (Forest Reference Emission Level) reporting to UNFCCC
- **Provincial BPBD Support Dashboards**: District-level air quality and deforestation monitoring dashboards for KLHK regional offices (Balai Besar)
- **Regulatory Evidence Package**: Satellite evidence dossiers to support PPNS (Civil Servant Investigators) in illegal deforestation enforcement actions
- **Capacity Building**: Annual training for KLHK remote sensing analysts on platform tools and NASA data products

### 4. Air Quality Co-monitoring Program

A priority initiative proposed for immediate activation:

- **Scope**: 147 KLHK AQMS stations integrated with MODIS MAIAC aerosol retrievals for bias-corrected PM2.5 estimates
- **Output**: Province-level AQI reports published daily, including wildfire smoke episode tracking (priority: Kalimantan, Sumatera peat fire season)
- **Public Delivery**: Real-time AQI map embedded on platform website and distributed via social media during high-risk events
- **Reporting**: Monthly air quality bulletin co-branded KLHK–Tropi, submitted to KLHK Direktorat Pengendalian Pencemaran Udara
