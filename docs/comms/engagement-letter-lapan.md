Building on the initial technical meetings conducted between **Tropi Climate Analytics** and **LAPAN (Lembaga Penerbangan dan Antariksa Nasional)** — now integrated within **BRIN (Badan Riset dan Inovasi Nasional)** — we write to formalize our data partnership agreement and advance joint satellite data utilization for Indonesian climate monitoring.

### 1. Strategic Alignment

LAPAN's unique position as Indonesia's primary space and remote sensing agency, combined with Tropi Climate Analytics' cloud-native big-data processing capabilities, creates a natural partnership for maximizing the scientific and policy value of Indonesian Earth observation assets. This partnership directly supports the **BRIN Strategic Plan 2025–2029** on national satellite data utilization and climate resilience research.

### 2. Platform Overview

Tropi Climate Analytics currently ingests and processes international NASA satellite data (Landsat, MODIS, SMAP, GPM, VIIRS). The LAPAN/BRIN partnership extends this with Indonesian sovereign satellite data assets:

- **LAPAN-A4 / Nusantara Earth observation** data integration into land cover monitoring workflows
- **Himawari-9 processed products** for real-time mesoscale convective system (MCS) tracking
- **SPOT-7 high-resolution imagery** for targeted deforestation ground-truth validation
- **SAR (Synthetic Aperture Radar)** data for cloud-penetrating deforestation and flood inundation mapping

### 3. Proposed LAPAN/BRIN Contribution

| Data Asset | Format | Delivery Mechanism | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| SPOT-7 processed imagery (priority hotspots) | GeoTIFF | LAPAN Data Portal API | On-demand |

### 4. Platform Benefits to LAPAN/BRIN

Tropi Climate Analytics commits to:

- **Joint Scientific Publications**: Co-authorship on peer-reviewed papers using LAPAN satellite data, targeting *Remote Sensing of Environment*, *International Journal of Remote Sensing*, and *Jurnal Penginderaan Jauh Indonesia*
- **LAPAN Data Portal Attribution**: All LAPAN-derived products credited on platform outputs and public communications
- **NASA Data Access Bridging**: Facilitating LAPAN researcher access to NASA CMR API data through our existing integration pipeline for joint analysis workflows
- **PhD/Research Collaboration**: Dedicated data access tier for BRIN researchers conducting climate and remote sensing studies
- **Capacity Exchange Program**: Tropi platform training for LAPAN remote sensing analysts; LAPAN satellite data training for Tropi data science team
- **Indonesian Satellite Integration Showcase**: Joint demonstration showcasing Indonesian sovereign satellite data in climate applications, presented at national BRIN/LAPAN scientific events

### 5. Priority Technical Initiative: SAR-Optical Fusion for Deforestation

A flagship joint project proposed for immediate development:

- **Objective**: Combine LAPAN SAR data with NASA Landsat optical imagery for all-weather, year-round deforestation mapping in Sumatera and Kalimantan (historically cloud-obscured during high-activity wet season)
- **Output**: Monthly SAR-optical fusion deforestation alert product; 20x improvement in cloudy-season coverage over optical-only approach
- **Publication Target**: Submit to *Remote Sensing* journal Q4 2026 with joint LAPAN-Tropi authorship
- **KLHK Delivery**: Provide product directly to KLHK as supporting evidence for forest enforcement
