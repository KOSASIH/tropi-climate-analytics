We write to formally advance the Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) between **Tropi Climate Analytics** and the **Badan Meteorologi, Klimatologi, dan Geofisika (BMKG)**, building on the shared commitment to strengthen Indonesia's climate monitoring and early warning capabilities through integrated satellite and ground-based data science.

### 1. Platform Overview

Tropi Climate Analytics is a cloud-based big-data analytics platform that integrates NASA satellite data (Landsat, MODIS, SMAP, GPM, VIIRS) with Indonesian ground observation networks to deliver:

- Real-time tropical deforestation monitoring and alerts
- Air quality index tracking at district resolution
- Sea surface temperature and marine hazard forecasting
- 24–72 hour precipitation nowcasting and seasonal outlooks
- Flood early warning for major river systems (Ciliwung, Brantas, Solo)

All platform outputs are published under **CC-BY 4.0**, with raw data stored in compliance with **PP No. 71/2019** on Indonesian data residency (AWS ap-southeast-3, Jakarta).

### 2. Proposed BMKG Contribution

Under the partnership framework (`docs/partnerships.md`), BMKG's proposed contributions include:

| Data Asset | Format | Delivery Mechanism | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Automatic Weather Station (AWS) observations | JSON/CSV | REST API | Real-time |
| Rain gauge network readings | NetCDF | Daily SFTP batch transfer | Daily |
| Seasonal climate forecasts | GRIB2 | SFTP | Monthly |
| Radar composite imagery | GeoTIFF | REST API | Sub-hourly |

### 3. Platform Benefits to BMKG

In exchange, Tropi Climate Analytics commits to:

- **Co-authorship** on all published weather alert and climate monitoring products derived from BMKG data
- **Joint BMKG–Tropi public communication campaigns** coordinated by COMM-ONNECT for seasonal forecasts and extreme weather events
- **Monthly technical synchronization meetings** between DATA-FLOW and BMKG technical teams
- **Dedicated BMKG portal** within the platform providing agency-specific dashboards and export tools
- **Capacity building**: quarterly workshops on satellite data integration for BMKG technical staff
- **BMKG branding** on all co-produced public products and media materials

### 4. Governance

The partnership operates under a **Quarterly Joint Steering Committee** chaired by Tropi Climate Analytics (CLIMATE-OS agent), with BMKG representation at Eselon II level. An Annual Stakeholder Workshop will review platform roadmap and data strategy.

### 5. Data Sharing Principles

All data exchange is governed by:
1. Platform outputs remain open under **CC-BY 4.0**
2. BMKG raw data usage governed by the MOU (individual data schedule attached)
3. All raw data stored exclusively on **AWS ap-southeast-3 (Jakarta)** per PP 71/2019
4. Joint publications acknowledge BMKG as contributing institution

### 6. Proposed Next Steps

1. **MOU Finalization** — We request a working-level meeting within 14 days to finalize MOU terms
2. **API Technical Handshake** — DATA-FLOW team ready for test API integration within 30 days of MOU signing
3. **Joint Campaign Kick-off** — First co-branded climate bulletin targeting the 2026 dry season (July–September)

We are prepared to present a full technical briefing to BMKG leadership at your earliest convenience. Please direct correspondence to the COMM-ONNECT coordination office at **partnerships@tropi-climate-analytics.id**.
