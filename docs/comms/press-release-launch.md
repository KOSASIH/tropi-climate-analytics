## Tropi Climate Analytics Launches Indonesia's First Integrated Satellite-Ground Climate Intelligence Platform

*Fusing NASA satellite data with BMKG, KLHK, and LAPAN networks to deliver real-time deforestation alerts, flood early warnings, and air quality monitoring across all 514 Indonesian districts*

---

**JAKARTA** — Tropi Climate Analytics today announced the public launch of its cloud-based climate intelligence platform, the first in Indonesia to fuse multiple NASA Earth observation satellite missions with national ground observation networks from BMKG, KLHK, and LAPAN into a single, open-access analytics infrastructure.

The platform delivers sub-hourly monitoring across five critical climate domains: tropical deforestation change detection, air quality and atmospheric monitoring, flood early warning, precipitation nowcasting, and sea surface temperature surveillance — covering all 34 Indonesian provinces and 514 districts.

"Indonesia is at the frontline of climate change, and our national decision-makers need access to the same quality of satellite data intelligence that global agencies have long used," said the Tropi Climate Analytics project team. "This platform closes that gap: real-time, open-access, PP 71/2019-compliant, and built specifically for Indonesian conditions."

### Platform Highlights

**Deforestation Monitoring**: Using Landsat 8/9 30-meter resolution imagery and MODIS NDVI time series, the platform provides weekly deforestation change detection for all Indonesian forest zones. SAR-optical fusion with LAPAN satellite data enables all-weather monitoring through cloud cover during the wet season — historically the most active period for illegal clearing in Kalimantan and Sumatera.

**Flood Early Warning**: Integration of GPM satellite precipitation estimates with SMAP soil moisture retrievals and BMKG rain gauge networks, processed through SWAT and VIC hydrological models, provides 6-to-24 hour flood early warning for 15 major river basins including the Ciliwung (Greater Jakarta), Brantas (East Java), and Solo rivers.

**Air Quality Monitoring**: Dual-source AQI calculation fuses MODIS MAIAC aerosol retrievals with 147 KLHK Automatic Air Quality Monitoring Station (AQMS) readings, producing bias-corrected PM2.5 and AQI maps every hour per Indonesian ISPU standards (PERMENLHK P.14/2020). Wildfire smoke episode tracking is operational for Kalimantan and Sumatera peat fire season.

**Open Data Commitment**: All platform outputs are published under Creative Commons CC-BY 4.0 license. Raw data is stored exclusively on AWS ap-southeast-3 (Jakarta) in full compliance with PP No. 71/2019 on data residency.

### Institutional Partnerships

The platform operates through formal data-sharing agreements with:
- **BMKG**: Primary ground observation data provider; co-authorship on weather alert products
- **KLHK**: Forest cover reference data and air quality monitoring network
- **LAPAN/BRIN**: Indonesian remote sensing and satellite data, including SPOT-7, SAR, and Himawari-9 products

"Our partnership with BMKG, KLHK, and LAPAN is foundational — Indonesian satellite and ground observation data paired with NASA's global constellation creates a monitoring capability far greater than either could deliver alone," said the platform's external relations team.

### Access and API

The platform provides:
- **Free public access** to current climate maps and district profiles
- **Government API tier** for BMKG, BNPB, KLHK, Bappenas, and provincial BPBD
- **Research API tier** with Python, R, and JavaScript SDKs and OpenAPI 3.0 documentation
- **Bulk historical data export** for academic institutions (20+ years of processed climate indices)

Interested parties may request access at **https://tropi-climate-analytics.id** or contact **partnerships@tropi-climate-analytics.id**.

### About Tropi Climate Analytics

Tropi Climate Analytics is an open-science climate intelligence platform built for Indonesia. The platform integrates NASA Earth observation data (Landsat, MODIS, SMAP, GPM, VIIRS) with Indonesian national ground networks to deliver actionable climate monitoring across deforestation, air quality, hydrology, marine, and atmospheric domains. All platform outputs are open access under CC-BY 4.0.
