### About Section
```
Tropi Climate Analytics is Indonesia's integrated climate intelligence platform — the first to fuse NASA Earth observation satellite data with national ground observation networks from BMKG, KLHK, and LAPAN into a unified, open-access analytics infrastructure.

The platform delivers real-time monitoring across five domains:

🌿 DEFORESTATION — Weekly Landsat 30m change detection across all 34 Indonesian provinces. Automated alerts for government agencies. SAR-optical fusion for all-weather monitoring.

💨 AIR QUALITY — PM2.5 and AQI tracking fused from MODIS aerosol and 147 KLHK AQMS stations, per Indonesian ISPU standards. Wildfire smoke episode mapping during peat fire season.

🌊 FLOOD EARLY WARNING — GPM satellite precipitation + SMAP soil moisture + SWAT hydrological models for 6–24 hour flood alerts on 15 major Indonesian river basins.

🌧️ PRECIPITATION FORECASTING — XGBoost nowcasting (24–72 hours) and Prophet seasonal outlooks. ENSO impact forecasts for Indonesian wet/dry season transitions.

🌡️ SEA SURFACE TEMPERATURE — Full EEZ coverage for marine heatwave detection and fisheries support.

All platform outputs are published under CC-BY 4.0. Data stored on AWS ap-southeast-3 (Jakarta) per PP No. 71/2019.

Institutional partners: BMKG · KLHK · LAPAN/BRIN · NASA
```
