### About the Webinar

Indonesia faces accelerating climate risks — from tropical deforestation and urban air pollution to catastrophic flooding and marine heatwaves. Yet for decades, real-time, district-level climate data has been inaccessible to the agencies and researchers who need it most.

**Tropi Climate Analytics changes that.**

At this inaugural briefing, we're opening the platform to Indonesia's climate decision-makers for the first time. Join us for live demonstrations of real-time deforestation monitoring, national air quality tracking, flood early warning, and more — all built on NASA satellite data fused with BMKG, KLHK, and LAPAN national ground networks.

**This webinar is for you if you are:**
- A government official at BMKG, KLHK, BNPB, Bappenas, KOMINFO, or provincial BPBD
- A climate researcher at a university, BRIN, or research center
- A developer or data scientist building environmental applications
- A journalist or policy analyst covering Indonesian climate issues
- Anyone committed to evidence-based climate action in Indonesia
